Dimension weights

Cross-enterprise default. Technical and enterprise readiness 20; business/platform coverage 20; AI and agentic applicability 12; delivery and operating economics 16; domain/use-case fit 24; adoption/value realization 8. Domain fit and platform breadth carry the most weight because this scenario needs a platform that reaches beyond one business domain. A single-domain preset (22/17/13/20/18/10) puts more weight on technical readiness and economics. Enter relative priorities; calculations normalize automatically.

Criterion weights

How the defaults were set. Technical, AI, operating-economics, domain-fit and adoption/value criteria use explicit priorities totaling 100%. Business criteria began as relative points: core ontology, integration and control domains = 10; major platform domains = 7; supporting domains = 4. Normalizing those points produces values such as 11.111% and 4.444%; the decimals are allocation results, not measurement precision. The same rubric appears in Methodology.
